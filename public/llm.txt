Nextloop Technologies

Nextloop Technologies is an India-based IT outsourcing and custom software development company, providing offshore development, staff augmentation, and dedicated development teams for AI, SaaS, and MVP projects, delivering from teams across India and the USA. Founded in 2020, the company is headquartered in Indore, India, with an additional office in the UK.

Core Services

- Custom Software Development: Tailored software solutions from internal tools to full-scale platforms.
- IT Staff Augmentation: Remote developers who embed with client teams and contribute from day one.
- Web Development: Responsive, high-performing websites and web applications.
- MVP Development: Focused, fast-to-market MVPs for early-stage startups.
- Cloud Services (DevOps, AWS, GCP, Azure): Scalable, secure cloud infrastructure and migration.
- Mobile Development: Native and cross-platform iOS/Android app development.
- UI/UX Development: Product design and interactive interface development.
- AI/ML: Generative AI, predictive systems, and AI-driven automation including chatbots.
- Digital Marketing Services: SEO, advertising, and social media strategy for traffic and lead generation.
