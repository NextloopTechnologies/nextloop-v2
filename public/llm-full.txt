NextLoop Technologies — Full System Documentation & Profile

Company Overview

Nextloop Technologies is an India-based IT outsourcing and custom software development company founded in 2020. Headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India, with an additional office in the UK, the company delivers offshore development through teams across India and the USA. Nextloop provides software development outsourcing, IT staff augmentation, MVP development, and generative AI development services, and has delivered 30+ projects with a team of 80+ senior-level developers.

Company Timeline

- 2019: Launched with a core engineering team of 4, delivering 6+ custom software projects.
- 2020: Transitioned to remote-first execution and achieved 90% client retention.
- 2021: Entered fintech and healthtech domains, grew the team by 60%, and launched cloud-native solution capabilities.
- 2022: Delivered 20+ scalable platforms, introduced DevOps & cloud optimization services.
- 2023: Partnered with high-growth startups and expanded staff augmentation services.
- 2024: Participated at GITEX Asia, expanded cross-border collaborations, and surpassed 50+ cumulative project deliveries.
- 2025: Driving digital transformation with AI, automation, blockchain, and scalable cloud ecosystems.
