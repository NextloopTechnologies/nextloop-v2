Most AI projects fail before a single line of code is written

Companies that rush into AI without a plan tend to make the same three mistakes. Each one is expensive to undo once you've started building.

Mistake 1

Feeding AI bad data

AI makes mistakes (hallucinates) if it reads messy, duplicated or outdated data. The result is confident answers your team can't trust.

How we prevent itWe check that your data is clean, complete and ready before anything is built.

Mistake 2

Putting private data into public AI

Pasting contracts, customer records or financials into public AI tools like ChatGPT can expose confidential information you can't take back.

How we prevent itWe plan private, secure systems that keep your data inside your own cloud.

Mistake 3

Building without a business goal

AI built because competitors are doing it rarely pays back. Budgets go to impressive demos that never reach daily use.

How we prevent itWe find the exact tasks where AI will actually save you time and money, and measure it.

What is the difference between AI consulting and AI development?

Consulting means we map out the plan, security and costs before anyone starts building. This prevents expensive mistakes. Development is the build itself, which your team or ours can do once the plan is approved.

How long does an AI Readiness Audit take?

Usually 2 to 4 weeks, depending on how much data and software your company uses.

Do we have to hire Nextloop to build the AI later?

No. We give you a complete technical plan. Your own IT team can build it, or you can hire us to do it.

How much does AI consulting cost?

The audit is a fixed-price engagement scoped to the size of your business and the systems involved. The first 30-minute call is free, and you'll receive a written price before any work starts.

What do you need from our team during the audit?

Around a few hours of interviews with the people who run the workflows, read-only access to sample data, and one technical contact from IT. We sign an NDA before any access is shared.

Will you need access to our live company data?

Not necessarily. We usually work with anonymised samples or read-only exports. If live access is needed, it's scoped, time-limited and covered by the NDA.

We're a mid-sized company. Is AI consulting only for large enterprises?

No. Smaller companies often benefit most, because one well-chosen automation can free up a large share of a small team's time. We scale the audit to your size.

How do you measure whether the AI is worth it?

Before any build, we agree on measurable targets such as hours saved per week, error rate or cost per transaction. The proof of concept has to hit those numbers before you invest in a full rollout.
